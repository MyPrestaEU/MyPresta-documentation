### 2.2 Dedicated Admin Screens & Controller Views

#### 2.2.1 AdminOpenGraphController (Back Office > Advanced Parameters > Open Graph Tags)

This screen allows you to manage specific Open Graph tags for individual pages or override default behavior. While the module automatically generates tags for products and categories, this section is useful for custom pages or fine-tuning.

- **Screen Name & Menu Location:** You can access this screen by navigating to **Advanced Parameters > Open Graph Tags** in your PrestaShop Back Office.
- **Available Actions & Settings:** This screen typically presents a grid (list) of custom Open Graph tag configurations you’ve added. You’ll find options to:
  - **Add New:** Create a new custom Open Graph tag set for a specific URL.
  - **Edit:** Modify an existing custom tag set.
  - **Delete:** Remove a custom tag set.
  - **Grid Columns:** Usually displays the URL, title, description, and image associated with the custom tag.
- **Effect of changes:**
  - **Adding a new entry:** When you add a new entry for a specific URL (e.g., a custom CMS page or a blog post URL), the module will use the title, description, and image you provide here instead of trying to automatically generate them when that specific URL is shared on Facebook.
  - **Editing an entry:** Updates the social preview for that specific URL.
  - **Deleting an entry:** Reverts the behavior for that URL back to the module’s automatic generation or the default homepage settings.
- **Recommended workflow for daily store management:**
  1. **Identify pages needing custom previews:** For most products and categories, the module works automatically. However, for custom CMS pages (e.g., “About Us,” “Contact Us,” blog posts if you have a blog integrated), you might want specific, engaging previews.
  2. **Click “Add New”** to create a new entry.
  3. **Enter the exact URL** of the page you want to customize.
  4. **Provide a compelling Title, Description, and upload a relevant Image** for that specific page.
  5. **Save** your changes.
  6. **Test:** Use Facebook’s [Sharing Debugger](https://developers.facebook.com/tools/debug/) to check how your page looks after making changes.

## 3. Merchant Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: My shares on Facebook still look bad, even after installing the module. What’s wrong?**
A:

1. **Check module activation:** Ensure “Enable Open Graph Tags” is set to YES in the module configuration.
2. **Clear PrestaShop Cache:** Go to **Advanced Parameters > Performance** and click “Clear cache” (or “Clear Smarty cache” in older versions). This is crucial for changes to take effect.
3. **Use Facebook Sharing Debugger:** This is your best friend! Go to [developers.facebook.com/tools/debug/](https://developers.facebook.com/tools/debug/), paste the URL you’re trying to share, and click “Debug.” It will show you exactly what tags Facebook is seeing and if there are any errors. You might need to click “Scrape Again” to force Facebook to re-read your page after making changes.
4. **Check for conflicts:** Another module or your theme might be adding conflicting Open Graph tags. The debugger will often highlight duplicate tags.

**Q: The image for my product shares is wrong or missing.**
A:

1. **Product Image:** Ensure your product has a main image assigned in its product settings.
2. **Image Size:** While Facebook can resize, very small or unusual aspect ratio images might be ignored. Aim for at least 600x315 pixels, ideally 1200x628 for optimal display.
3. **Cache:** Clear PrestaShop cache and use the Facebook Sharing Debugger to “Scrape Again.”

**Q: How do I get a Facebook App ID or Admin ID?**
A:

1. **Facebook App ID:** Go to [developers.facebook.com](https://developers.facebook.com/), log in, and create a new App. Follow the steps to get your App ID. It’s a numerical string.
2. **Facebook Admin ID:** This is your personal Facebook User ID. You can find it by going to your Facebook profile, clicking on your profile picture, and looking at the URL in your browser (e.g., `facebook.com/profile.php?id=YOUR_USER_ID`).

**Q: Does this module work with custom themes?**
A: Yes, the module injects tags directly into the `<head>` section of your HTML, which is standard practice and should work with any well-coded PrestaShop theme. If you encounter issues, it’s usually a caching problem or a conflict with another module.

**Q: My custom page (e.g., a blog post) isn’t showing the right preview.**
A: Use the **Advanced Parameters > Open Graph Tags** section in the Back Office. Add a new entry for the exact URL of your custom page and manually specify the desired title, description, and image. Remember to clear cache and use the Facebook Sharing Debugger afterward.
