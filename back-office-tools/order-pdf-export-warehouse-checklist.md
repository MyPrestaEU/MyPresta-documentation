# Order PDF Export & Warehouse Checklist

## 1. Requirements & Compatibility

- **Supported PrestaShop Versions**: PrestaShop 1.7.x, 8.x, 9.x
- **PHP Compatibility**: PHP 7.1 - 8.5
- **Hosting Requirements**: Standard PHP extensions (cURL, JSON, OpenSSL) are required, which are typically available on most PrestaShop-compatible hosting environments.

## 2. Complete Configuration Walkthrough (Every Setting Grouped by Screen)

### 2.1 Module Configuration Page (Back Office > Modules > Configure)

To access this screen, navigate to **Modules > Module Manager**, search for “Order PDF Export & Warehouse Checklist”, and click “Configure”.

#### **PDF Template**

- **What this setting does:** This allows you to choose the visual design and layout for the PDF documents generated by the module. You can select from a variety of pre-designed templates.
- **Effect of changing this setting:**
  - *When set to ‘Clean’:* Your PDFs will have a minimalist, modern look.
  - *When set to ‘Warehouse Big’:* The PDF will be optimized for warehouse use, potentially with larger fonts and clear sections for picking.
  - *When set to ‘Retro’:* Your PDFs will adopt a vintage-inspired design.
- **Recommended value:** Start with ‘Clean’ or ‘Warehouse Big’ depending on your primary use case (customer packing slip vs. internal picking list). Experiment to find the best fit for your brand and operational needs.
- **Merchant scenario / example:** If you want your packing slips to reflect a modern, professional brand image, you might choose ‘Clean’ or ‘Elegant Serif’. If your main goal is to provide a highly readable picking list for warehouse staff, ‘Warehouse Big’ might be more suitable.

#### **Enable MyPresta.eu Updates** (mypresta\_updates)

- **What this setting does:** This controls whether the module can check for and notify you about available updates from MyPresta.eu.
- **Effect of changing this setting:**
  - *When enabled (Yes):* The module will periodically check for new versions, ensuring you stay up-to-date with the latest features, bug fixes, and compatibility improvements. You might see notifications in your PrestaShop back office when an update is available.
  - *When disabled (No):* The module will not check for updates. You will need to manually check your MyPresta.eu account for new versions.
- **Recommended value:** **Yes**. Keeping modules updated is crucial for security, performance, and compatibility with new PrestaShop versions.
- **Merchant scenario / example:** Enabling this ensures you’re always running the most stable and feature-rich version of the module without having to constantly check manually.

#### **Update Version** (update\_) & **Update Version Value** (updatev\_)

- **What these settings do:** These are internal settings used by the module to manage its update mechanism. They store information about the latest available version or the version currently installed.
- **Effect of changing this setting:** These are **not meant to be changed manually** by the merchant. Altering them could interfere with the module’s ability to check for and apply updates correctly.
- **Recommended value:** Leave these settings as they are. They are managed automatically by the module.
- **Merchant scenario / example:** You will typically not interact with these settings. They are part of the module’s internal update logic.

### 2.2 Dedicated Admin Screens & Controller Views

The module adds new menu items under **Orders** in your PrestaShop Back Office, allowing you to manage your warehouse checklist categories and elements.

#### **Screen Name: Checklist Categories**

- **Menu Location:** Back Office > Orders > **Checklist Categories**
- **Available Actions & Settings:** This screen allows you to create, edit, and delete categories for your warehouse checklist. Think of categories as logical groupings for your checklist items (e.g., “Packaging Quality,” “Product Verification,” “Shipping Documents”).
  - **Adding a New Category:** Click “Add new category” to define a new group. You’ll typically provide a name for the category (e.g., “Product Inspection”).
  - **Editing/Deleting Categories:** You can modify existing categories or remove them if they are no longer needed.
- **Effect of changes:**
  - *Adding a category:* Creates a new section under which you can later add specific checklist elements.
  - *Modifying a category:* Changes the name or properties of an existing category.
  - *Deleting a category:* Removes the category and any associated checklist elements from the system. This will no longer appear on your generated PDFs.
- **Recommended workflow for daily store management:**
  1. Start by defining broad categories that reflect your warehouse process. For example: “Order Accuracy,” “Packaging Integrity,” “Documentation.”
  2. Ensure category names are clear and descriptive for your staff.

#### **Screen Name: Checklist Elements**

- **Menu Location:** Back Office > Orders > **Checklist Elements**
- **Available Actions & Settings:** This screen is where you define the actual items that will appear on your warehouse checklist within the PDF. Each element can be assigned to a category you created previously.
  - **Adding a New Element:** Click “Add new element.” You’ll specify:
    - **Name:** The text of the checklist item (e.g., “All items present and accounted for?”).
    - **Category:** Assign it to one of the categories you defined (e.g., “Order Accuracy”).
    - **Active:** Whether this element should appear on the checklist.
  - **Editing/Deleting Elements:** Modify or remove individual checklist items.
- **Effect of changes:**
  - *Adding an element:* The new item will appear on the generated PDF checklist under its assigned category.
  - *Modifying an element:* Changes the text or category of an existing checklist item.
  - *Deleting an element:* Removes the item from the checklist on the PDFs.
- **Recommended workflow for daily store management:**
  1. Once categories are set up, populate them with specific, actionable checklist items.
  2. Examples: “Product quantity matches order?”, “Fragile items properly cushioned?”, “Shipping label correct?”, “Invoice included?”.
  3. Regularly review and update your checklist elements to reflect changes in your products, packaging, or shipping procedures.

#### **Screen Name: Order List (with Export Button)**

- **Menu Location:** Back Office > Orders > **Orders**
- **Available Actions & Settings:** This module integrates directly into your standard PrestaShop Orders list. You will find new buttons or options to generate PDFs for individual orders or potentially in bulk.
  - **Individual Order Export:** When viewing a specific order in the Back Office, you will see a new button (e.g., “Export PDF” or “Print Picking List”) that allows you to generate the customized PDF for that order.
  - **Bulk Order Export:** (Depending on the module version and PrestaShop version) You might be able to select multiple orders from the order list and generate PDFs for them all at once.
- **Effect of changes:** Clicking these buttons will download a PDF document to your computer, formatted according to your chosen template and including your custom checklist.
- **Recommended workflow for daily store management:**
  1. After an order is placed and validated, navigate to the Orders list.
  2. Open the specific order you need to fulfill.
  3. Click the “Export PDF” button to generate the picking list/packing slip with the checklist.
  4. Print the PDF and hand it to your warehouse staff for fulfillment.
  5. Staff use the PDF to pick items, verify accuracy using the checklist, and potentially collect signatures before packing.

## 3. Merchant Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: The PDF looks strange or has formatting issues. What should I do?**
A:

1. **Clear Smarty Cache:** Go to **Advanced Parameters > Performance** in your PrestaShop Back Office. Scroll down to “Cache” and click “Clear cache.” Sometimes, old template files can cause display issues.
2. **Try a Different PDF Template:** In the module’s configuration, try selecting a different “PDF Template” to see if the issue is specific to one layout.
3. **Check for Theme Conflicts:** If you’re using a heavily customized theme, it might have CSS or JS that interferes. Temporarily switch to the default PrestaShop theme (e.g., Classic) and re-test. If it works, your theme might require adjustments.
4. **Contact Support:** If problems persist, gather screenshots and details, then contact MyPresta.eu support.

**Q: My custom checklist items are not appearing on the PDF.**
A:

1. **Check “Checklist Categories”:** Ensure you have created categories under **Orders > Checklist Categories**.
2. **Check “Checklist Elements”:** Verify that you have added elements under **Orders > Checklist Elements** and that they are assigned to an active category. Also, make sure the “Active” switch for each element is set to “Yes”.
3. **Module Configuration:** Ensure the module is properly configured and saved.

**Q: I don’t see the “Export PDF” button on my order pages.**
A:

1. **Module Installed & Enabled:** Double-check that the module is installed and enabled in **Modules > Module Manager**.
2. **Permissions:** Ensure your employee profile has the necessary permissions to access and use this module. Go to **Advanced Parameters > Team > Profiles** and check the permissions for the profile assigned to your employee account.
3. **PrestaShop Version:** Confirm your PrestaShop version is compatible (1.7.x, 8.x, 9.x).
4. **Clear Cache:** Clear your PrestaShop cache (**Advanced Parameters > Performance**).

**Q: Can I customize the PDF templates further (e.g., add my logo, change fonts)?**
A: Yes, but this requires technical knowledge. The module provides several templates, but for deep customization (like adding your specific logo, changing fonts, or altering the layout beyond what the module settings offer), you would need to:

1. **Identify the Template File:** Locate the `.tpl` file for the chosen PDF template within the module’s `pdf/en/` directory.
2. **Edit the Template:** This involves editing Smarty template code and potentially HTML/CSS. This is a task for a webmaster or developer.
3. **Consider a Custom Template:** For significant changes, it’s often better to create a new custom template rather than modifying the core module files, to ensure your changes aren’t overwritten during module updates.

**Q: The module is not checking for updates.**
A:

1. **Enable Update Check:** Go to the module’s configuration page (**Modules > Module Manager > Configure** for “Order PDF Export & Warehouse Checklist”) and ensure “Enable MyPresta.eu Updates” is set to “Yes”.
2. **Server Connectivity:** Ensure your server has outbound internet access to connect to MyPresta.eu’s update servers. This might involve checking firewall settings or proxy configurations with your hosting provider.
