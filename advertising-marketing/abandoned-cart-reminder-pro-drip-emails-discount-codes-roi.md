# Abandoned Cart Reminder Pro – drip emails, discount codes & ROI

## 1. Requirements & Compatibility

- **Supported PrestaShop Versions**: PrestaShop 1.7.x, 8.x, 9.x
- **PHP Compatibility**: PHP 7.1 - 8.5 (The module is written with PHP 5.6+ in mind, but PrestaShop 9.x requires PHP 8.1+ for the store itself. We recommend using the highest stable PHP version compatible with your PrestaShop installation, up to 8.5.)
- **Hosting Requirements**: Standard PHP extensions like cURL, JSON, and OpenSSL are required. Your hosting environment must also support cron jobs (scheduled tasks) for the module to function correctly, as it processes reminders in the background.

## 2. Complete Configuration Walkthrough (Every Setting Grouped by Screen)

The “Abandoned Cart Reminder Pro” module offers a comprehensive set of tools to manage your campaigns. You’ll primarily interact with its dedicated menu items in the PrestaShop Back Office.

### 2.1 Module Configuration Page (Back Office > Modules > Configure)

While the module has dedicated admin screens, the initial “Configure” button from the Module Manager might lead to a basic setup or redirection. The module leverages PrestaShop’s `PS_SSL_ENABLED` configuration key.

- **What this setting does:** This setting isn’t directly controlled by the module but is a core PrestaShop setting that indicates whether your store uses HTTPS (secure connection). The module relies on this to generate correct, secure links in your reminder emails.
- **Effect of changing this setting:**
  - *When `PS_SSL_ENABLED` is YES:* All links generated by the module (e.g., to abandoned carts, opt-out pages) will use `https://`, ensuring a secure and trusted experience for your customers.
  - *When `PS_SSL_ENABLED` is NO:* Links might be generated using `http://`, which is less secure and can trigger browser warnings, potentially reducing customer trust and conversion rates.
- **Recommended value:** **YES**. Your store should always use SSL for security and SEO reasons. Ensure your entire store is running on HTTPS.
- **Merchant scenario / example:** If your customers receive an email with an `http://` link to their cart, their browser might show a “Not Secure” warning. This can make them hesitant to click the link or complete the purchase, even if your checkout is secure. Always use HTTPS.

### 2.2 Dedicated Admin Screens & Controller Views

Once the module is installed, you’ll find a new menu item in your PrestaShop Back Office, typically under **Marketing** or a dedicated section for “Abandoned Cart Reminder Pro”. This menu will lead to several sub-sections:

#### 2.2.1 Campaigns (Back Office > Abandoned Cart Reminder Pro > Campaigns)

This is where you create, manage, and optimize your abandoned cart reminder campaigns.

- **Screen Name & Menu Location:** **Abandoned Cart Reminder Pro > Campaigns**
- **Available Actions & Settings:**
  - **Campaign List:** A table showing all your active and inactive campaigns, including their name, status, and key statistics.
  - **Add New Campaign:** Click to create a new multi-step email sequence.
    - **Campaign Name:** A descriptive name for your internal reference (e.g., “Standard 2-Step Reminder,” “High Value Cart Discount”).
    - **Targeting Rules:**
      - **Minimum Cart Value:** Set a minimum total value for a cart to be eligible for this campaign.
        - *Effect:* Carts below this value will not receive emails from this campaign.
        - *Merchant Scenario:* Create a campaign specifically for high-value carts, perhaps offering a larger discount.
      - **Customer Groups:** Target specific customer groups (e.g., “Visitors,” “Customers,” “Wholesalers”).
        - *Effect:* Only customers belonging to the selected groups will receive emails.
        - *Merchant Scenario:* Send different reminders to new visitors versus loyal customers, or exclude B2B customers if they have a different purchasing process.
      - **Products in Cart:** Target carts containing specific products or products from certain categories.
        - *Effect:* Only carts with the specified products will trigger the campaign.
        - *Merchant Scenario:* Create a campaign for customers who abandoned a cart with a specific high-margin product.
    - **Campaign Steps (Drip Emails):** Define the sequence of emails to be sent.
      - **Delay:** How long after cart abandonment should this email be sent (e.g., 1 hour, 24 hours, 3 days).
        - *Effect:* Controls the timing of your follow-up.
        - *Merchant Scenario:* Send a quick reminder after 1 hour, a stronger one with a discount after 24 hours, and a final one after 3 days.
      - **Email Subject:** The subject line of the email (supports multiple languages).
      - **Email Content (Email Builder):** Design the body of your email using a rich text editor. You can include dynamic placeholders like customer name, cart link, product list, and discount codes.
      - **Discount Code:** Option to generate a unique, one-time discount code for this specific email.
        - *Effect:* A unique voucher code is created and included in the email, encouraging conversion.
        - *Merchant Scenario:* Offer a 10% discount in the second reminder email to incentivize completion.
      - **A/B Testing:** Create variations of your email subject or content to test which performs better.
        - *Effect:* The module will split sends between variations and track conversion rates, allowing you to optimize your campaigns.
        - *Merchant Scenario:* Test two different subject lines to see which gets a higher open rate.
  - **Edit/Duplicate/Delete Campaigns:** Manage existing campaigns. Duplicating is useful for creating similar campaigns quickly.
- **Effect of changes:** Changes here directly impact which customers receive reminder emails, when they receive them, what content they see, and whether they receive discount codes. Well-configured campaigns can significantly increase your store’s recovery rate.
- **Recommended workflow for daily store management:**
  1. Start with a simple 2-step campaign: a reminder after 1-2 hours (no discount) and another after 24 hours (with a small discount).
  2. Monitor your Dashboard (see 4.2.2) to see which campaigns are performing best.
  3. Experiment with A/B testing on subject lines or discount amounts to optimize conversion rates.
  4. Consider creating targeted campaigns for specific customer segments or high-value products.

#### 2.2.4 Settings (Back Office > Abandoned Cart Reminder Pro > Settings)

This screen contains global settings that affect the overall behavior of the module.

- **Screen Name & Menu Location:** **Abandoned Cart Reminder Pro > Settings**
- **Available Actions & Settings:**
  - **General Settings:**
    - **Enable Module:** Turn the entire module on or off.
      - *Effect:* When disabled, no carts will be tracked, and no emails will be sent.
      - *Recommended:* YES, unless you are temporarily disabling it for maintenance.
    - **Minimum Cart Value for Tracking:** Set a global minimum cart value for any cart to be considered “abandoned” and tracked by the module.
      - *Effect:* Carts below this value will be ignored by all campaigns.
      - *Merchant Scenario:* Avoid sending reminders for very small carts where the recovery effort might not be worth it.
    - **Time to Consider Cart Abandoned:** How long after the last cart activity should a cart be considered abandoned (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour).
      - *Effect:* This determines when the module starts counting the delay for the first reminder email.
      - *Recommended:* 30-60 minutes. Too short might annoy customers, too long might miss the window of opportunity.
    - **Anti-Spam Limits:**
      - **Max Emails per Customer:** Limit the total number of abandoned cart emails a single customer can receive over a period (e.g., 3 emails per 30 days).
        - *Effect:* Prevents customers from being overwhelmed by too many emails, reducing unsubscribe rates.
        - *Recommended:* Set reasonable limits to maintain a positive customer experience.
      - **Min Time Between Emails:** Ensure a minimum delay between any two emails sent to the same customer.
        - *Effect:* Prevents multiple emails from different campaigns being sent too close together.
    - **GDPR / Opt-Out Settings:**
      - **Enable Opt-Out Link:** Include a link in reminder emails allowing customers to unsubscribe from future reminders.
        - *Effect:* Crucial for GDPR compliance and respecting customer privacy.
        - *Recommended:* YES.
      - **Opt-Out Page Content:** Customize the text displayed on the opt-out confirmation page.
    - **Cron Job URL:** Displays the exact URL you need to set up as a cron job on your server.
      - *Effect:* This URL triggers the module’s background processing, sending emails, and updating statistics.
      - *Recommended:* Copy this URL and configure your server’s cron job to run it every 5-15 minutes.
  - **Email Settings:**
    - **Sender Name & Email:** The name and email address that will appear as the sender of your reminder emails.
      - *Effect:* Impacts email deliverability and how customers perceive your brand.
      - *Recommended:* Use a recognizable store name and a professional email address (e.g., `sales@yourstore.com`, `noreply@yourstore.com`).
    - **Email Template:** Choose a base template for your reminder emails (if multiple are available).
      - *Effect:* Determines the overall design and layout of your emails.
  - **Advanced Settings:**
    - **Debug Mode:** Enable for detailed logging (for developers).
      - *Effect:* Provides more verbose information in the logs, useful for troubleshooting.
      - *Recommended:* NO, unless specifically asked by support.
- **Effect of changes:** These settings control the fundamental behavior of the module, from when it tracks carts to how it respects customer privacy and sends emails. Incorrect settings can lead to missed opportunities or a poor customer experience.
- **Recommended workflow for daily store management:** Review these settings during initial setup and periodically to ensure they align with your store’s policies and marketing strategy. Pay close attention to anti-spam and GDPR settings.

## 3. Merchant Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: My customers aren’t receiving reminder emails. What should I check?**
A1:

- **Cron Job:** This is the most common issue. Ensure your cron job is correctly set up on your server and running regularly (every 5-15 minutes). The exact URL is found in **Abandoned Cart Reminder Pro > Settings**.
- **Module Enabled:** Check **Abandoned Cart Reminder Pro > Settings** to ensure the module is globally enabled.
- **Campaigns Active:** Verify that your campaigns are enabled and have active steps in **Abandoned Cart Reminder Pro > Campaigns**.
- **Email Configuration:** Check your PrestaShop email settings (Advanced Parameters > E-mail) to ensure your store can send emails generally.
- **Spam Filters:** Ask a test customer to check their spam/junk folder. Ensure your sender email address is professional and not blacklisted.
- **Logs:** Go to **Abandoned Cart Reminder Pro > Logs** to see if emails are being queued, sent, or if there are any error messages.

**Q2: Why are some abandoned carts not triggering a campaign?**
A2:

- **Minimum Cart Value:** Check the global “Minimum Cart Value for Tracking” in **Abandoned Cart Reminder Pro > Settings** and the specific “Minimum Cart Value” for your campaigns in **Abandoned Cart Reminder Pro > Campaigns**. Carts below these values will be ignored.
- **Time to Consider Cart Abandoned:** Ensure the time set in **Abandoned Cart Reminder Pro > Settings** has passed since the last cart activity.
- **Customer Groups/Product Targeting:** If your campaign has specific customer group or product targeting, ensure the abandoned cart meets those criteria.
- **Anti-Spam Limits:** The customer might have reached the “Max Emails per Customer” or “Min Time Between Emails” limits set in **Abandoned Cart Reminder Pro > Settings**.
- **Customer Opt-Out:** The customer might have previously opted out of receiving reminders. Check the logs.

**Q3: How do I make sure the reminder emails look good on mobile devices?**
A3: The module uses responsive email templates. However, the content you add in the email builder can affect this.

- **Keep it simple:** Avoid overly complex layouts or very wide images.
- **Test:** Always send a test email to yourself and view it on different devices (desktop, tablet, phone) before activating a campaign.
- **PrestaShop Theme:** Ensure your main PrestaShop theme is mobile-responsive, as the links in the emails will lead back to your store.

**Q4: Can I translate the email content and subject lines?**
A4: Yes! When editing a campaign step, you’ll see language tabs for the subject and email content. You can provide different translations for each language installed in your PrestaShop store. This is crucial for international stores.

**Q5: I cleared my Smarty cache, and now something looks off. What happened?**
A5: Clearing the Smarty cache (under **Advanced Parameters > Performance**) is a common troubleshooting step for display issues. While generally safe, sometimes it can temporarily affect how modules render their content until the cache is rebuilt. If you notice display issues after clearing the cache, try refreshing the page a few times or re-saving the module’s configuration (even without changing anything) to force a cache rebuild for the module. This module uses its own caching mechanisms, so clearing Smarty cache should not directly impact its core functionality, but rather the display of its admin interface.

## 4. Developer & Webmaster Guide (Technical Architecture & Code Map)

This section provides a deep dive into the technical structure of the “Abandoned Cart Reminder Pro” module for developers, webmasters, and technical agencies.

### 4.1 Architecture Overview

The module follows a robust, event-driven architecture adhering to PrestaShop’s MVC pattern where appropriate, but also leveraging a custom service container and autoloading for better organization and testability. It heavily relies on cron jobs for background processing, ensuring that email sending and cart tracking do not impact front-office performance. Key components include:

- **Cart Tracking:** Hooks into `ActionCartSave` and `ActionValidateOrder` to monitor cart status.
- **Campaign Engine:** Manages multi-step email sequences, targeting, and discount generation.
- **Queue Processor:** Handles the asynchronous sending of emails via cron.
- **Analytics & Attribution:** Tracks recovery rates and revenue.
- **Admin Controllers:** Dedicated controllers for managing campaigns, viewing logs, and configuring settings.
- **GDPR Compliance:** Includes an opt-out mechanism.

### 4.2 Controllers (`controllers/`)

#### Front-Office Controllers

These controllers handle specific front-office interactions, primarily for cron execution, tracking, and opt-out.

- `controllers/front/cron.php`
  - **Class Name:** `CartRemProCronModuleFrontController` (extends `ModuleFrontController`)
  - **Responsibility:** This is the primary endpoint for the module’s cron job. When accessed, it triggers the `CartRemProQueueProcessor` to process the queue of abandoned carts, send scheduled emails, and update statistics. It’s designed to be called by a server-side cron task, not directly by users.
- `controllers/front/index.php`
  - **Class Name:** `CartRemProIndexModuleFrontController` (extends `ModuleFrontController`)
  - **Responsibility:** A standard PrestaShop practice to prevent direct directory listing. Typically redirects to the shop’s homepage or displays a blank page.
- `controllers/front/optout.php`
  - **Class Name:** `CartRemProOptoutModuleFrontController` (extends `ModuleFrontController`)
  - **Responsibility:** Handles customer requests to opt-out from abandoned cart reminders. It processes the `optout` action, updates the customer’s preference, and displays a confirmation page (`optout.tpl`).
- `controllers/front/track.php`
  - **Class Name:** `CartRemProTrackModuleFrontController` (extends `ModuleFrontController`)
  - **Responsibility:** Used for tracking email opens and link clicks within the reminder emails. It records these events in the module’s logs, allowing for analytics on email engagement.

#### Back-Office Admin Controllers

These controllers manage the module’s interface in the PrestaShop Back Office.

- `controllers/admin/AdminCartRemProCampaignsController.php`
  - **Tab Name:** Abandoned Cart Reminder Pro > Campaigns
  - **Responsibility:** Manages the creation, editing, duplication, and deletion of abandoned cart campaigns. It handles the form submission for campaign details, steps, targeting rules, and A/B testing configurations. It interacts with `CartRemProCampaign` and `CartRemProCampaignStep` classes.
- `controllers/admin/AdminCartRemProDashboardController.php`
  - **Tab Name:** Abandoned Cart Reminder Pro > Dashboard
  - **Responsibility:** Displays the module’s performance metrics, including abandoned carts, recovered carts, attributed revenue, and ROI. It aggregates data from `CartRemProAnalytics` and `CartRemProAbandoned` to present a comprehensive overview.
- `controllers/admin/AdminCartRemProLogsController.php`
  - **Tab Name:** Abandoned Cart Reminder Pro > Logs
  - **Responsibility:** Provides a detailed log of all module activities, such as cart abandonments, email sends, opens, clicks, and recoveries. It allows filtering and searching through these logs for troubleshooting and auditing.
- `controllers/admin/AdminCartRemProSettingsController.php`
  - **Tab Name:** Abandoned Cart Reminder Pro > Settings
  - **Responsibility:** Manages the global configuration settings for the module, including enabling/disabling, anti-spam limits, GDPR options, and cron job URL display. It interacts with PrestaShop’s `Configuration` class.
- `controllers/admin/index.php`
  - **Responsibility:** Standard PrestaShop security file to prevent direct directory listing.
